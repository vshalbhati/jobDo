6. Company portals (mode 2)

    

      Jobs whose Apply button sends you to the employer's own site. Turning this
      on also drops the Easy Apply filter from the search, so both kinds of job
      come back. These applications are slower and less predictable than Easy
      Apply, which is why they get their own, smaller budget.
    

    

      
      
    

    

      
    


    
Recognised systems

    

      Auto means a public one-page form it can fill and submit.
      Assisted means the site wants an account or a multi-step wizard: it
      fills in what it can, leaves the tab open and moves on.
    

    


    
Sites it does not recognise

    

      
    

    

    
    

    
Cover letter

    
Used when a form asks for one. Placeholders: {{title}} {{company}} {{name}} {{years}} {{skills}}. Leave blank and any application that requires a cover letter is handed to you instead.
